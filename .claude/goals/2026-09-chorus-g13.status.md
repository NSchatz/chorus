- 2026-10-01: tracks (design envelope `/cache/tmp/chorus-g13/design.md`, coordinator's): three

  code survey first; then four build tracks in parallel: `lowlat-wire` (the datagram, FEC, Rust
  and C, the simulator budget; foundation), `capture` (rate matching and refusals on the hub),
  `cec` (a new `crates/cec` and the hub's audio-system role), `theater` (5.1 maps, A/V trim, TV

  end-to-end tests, the bench packet). Gate and merge through goal 12's coordinator script copied
  to `/cache/tmp/chorus-g13/` (now with `timeout 3600` on `make gate`).
- 2026-10-01: the TV path's audio does not ride the slot grid: a TV input played into one wired
  room goes through a relay on the server (stamp = capture stamp + L_tv + trim); grouped or Wi-Fi
  playback falls back to the line-in path of ADR 0079. Reason: the grid's 20 ms chunks and the
  endpoints' fixed 180 ms playout latency cannot meet BRIEF §2.2's lip-sync window.
- 2026-10-01: UDP datagram keys are fresh random per offer, carried inside the encrypted TCP
  session (`0x16 low_latency_offer`), not derived from Noise internals: the Noise code exposes no
  key export and its nonces are strictly sequential (survey); a sent key keeps K62's encryption
  without changing the handshake.
