## Side findings (not asked, but load-bearing)

- TAS5805M accepts 32-96 kHz only ("fS Supported input sample rates 32 96 kHz"); TAS5825M 32-192 kHz; both "No MCLK Required" (datasheets above). Research-endpoint-hardware.md's table is right.
- TAS5805M is not a drop-in power equivalent: its headline is "2 × 23 W in 2.0 Mode (8-Ω, 21 V, THD+N=1%)", not 2x38 W. BRIEF §5.5 calls it the "close sibling" in the same sentence as "2x38 W at 24 V".
- ESP32-P4 I2S: default source is "Auto select maximum clock source" (not APLL); PLL_F160M is v3-silicon only (esp32p4 clk_tree_defs.h).
- ES228GP product URL moved to `/us/business-networking/omada-switch-agile/es228gp/`; the budget gap in research-platform-network.md and survey-homelab.md can be closed at 250 W.
