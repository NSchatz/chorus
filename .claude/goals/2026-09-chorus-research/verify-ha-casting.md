### CORRECT

1. **Claim 5 wording** (research-ha-integration.md, R7.9 and 1.1): say "`uv pip install`ed into the
   image's system site-packages (Dockerfile sets `UV_SYSTEM_PYTHON=true`) when missing, at integration
   load; skipped under `--skip-pip`; lost on container recreate". "pip-installed" is loose.
2. **Claim 7 CI recipe** (research-ha-integration.md R1.1, the `ha-hassfest` gate): "copy into
   `homeassistant/components/chorus` (drop `version`)" is not enough. Once copied, hassfest applies
   the CORE manifest schema, so `documentation` must be rewritten to a
   `https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/...` URL (core_documentation_url), and the
   strict-typing validator requires the domain in the checkout's `.strict-typing`. Expect CODEOWNERS
   to need an entry too. The gate script must patch these in the throwaway checkout, and the goal
   should say so. The tier/skip claim itself stands.
3. **Claim 11 attribution**: "about 2028 (baseline) / 2031 (extensions)" is Wikipedia's reading of
   a patent list ("Based on the list of patents from the SEC terms"), not a Via LA statement. Cite
   Wikipedia for the dates and Via LA only for "licence needed for decoder products". MP3 wording:
   Wikipedia says "became patent-free in the United States on 16 April 2017" under the
   longest-patent measure; keep that hedge.
4. **Claim 9 phrasing**: "AVTransport needed for HTTP-pull" is an inference from ConnectionManager:1
   section 5.1.1.2/5.1.1.4 plus MediaRenderer:1 section 2.2.1; the CM:1 v1.0.1 Table 10 the template
   refers to has no explicit "requires AVTransport" column. Label it inference with those section
   numbers, as the research already half does.
5. **Claim 6 follow-through**: HA 2026.9.4 exists (pytest-homeassistant-custom-component 0.13.367,
   2026-09-27, pins `homeassistant==2026.9.4`). If the homelab pin moves, the test pin moves with it.
