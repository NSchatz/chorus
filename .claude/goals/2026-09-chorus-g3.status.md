  all" is itself a gate step.

  actionlint 1.7.12 (aqua, sha256 per platform in `mise.lock` from `mise lock`), and yamllint

  2.22.0 (2026-09-19) but ships no wheel; 2.17.1 is what installs rootless. CI installs them with
