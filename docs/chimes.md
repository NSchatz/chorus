# The chimes

The built-in alarm and notification sounds (K80's "built-in chimes, licence recorded"). Every one
is **generated by chorus's own code** (`crates/schedule/src/chime.rs`): additive synthesis from
the partial tables in that file, with no recording, sample or third-party sound anywhere in it.
The rendered audio is chorus's own work under the repository's licence, **MIT OR Apache-2.0**
(`LICENSE-MIT`, `LICENSE-APACHE`). There is nothing to attribute.

A group plays one as the source `chime:<name>`. The decision record
is `docs/decisions/0072-the-schedule-library.md`. The names have one home, `CHIMES` in that file:
the server reads them there and lists them in the state message as `chimes`
(`docs/control-plane.md`, "The state message"; ADR 0194), so a screen that offers an alarm's
chime takes the list from the state and keeps none of its own.

| Name | Length | What it is | Built from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bell` | 3.0 s | one bell strike on A4 (440 Hz) | seven partials at 0.5, 1, 1.2, 1.5, 2, 3 and 4 times the fundamental (hum, prime, minor-third tierce, quint, nominal, superquint, octave nominal), decay constants 3.0 s down to 0.3 s |
| `ding-dong` | 2.8 s | the doorbell: E5 (659.255 Hz), then C5 (523.251 Hz) 650 ms later | a struck bar's three modes at 1, 2.756 and 5.404 times the fundamental, decay constants 1.6, 0.6 and 0.25 s |
| `triad` | 1.8 s | a soft notification: C5, E5, G5 (783.991 Hz), 150 ms apart | partials at 1, 2 and 3 times the fundamental, decay constants 0.9, 0.45 and 0.25 s |

Every chime, at any rate from 8 to 384 kHz, in `pcm_s16le`, `pcm_s24le` or `pcm_f32le`, on 1 to 8
channels (the same signal on each):

- starts at zero: each strike opens with a 5 ms linear attack (ASSUMED), so it does not click;
- ends at digital silence: a 300 ms linear release (ASSUMED) reaches exactly zero on the last
  frame, in every format;
- peaks at exactly half of full scale, -6.02 dBFS, at or below the ASSUMED -6 dBFS ceiling;
- is bit-identical on every machine: no libm function is called (the sine and the exponential
  are computed from IEEE 754 basic operations), and the phase is exact integer arithmetic.

`crates/schedule/tests/chimes.rs` pins the 48 kHz mono `pcm_s16le` render of each with an FNV-1a
64 digest. To listen to one before changing it, `cargo run -p chorus-schedule --example
chime_wav -- bell 48000 /scratch/bell.wav`; a changed digest is a changed sound and is noted here.
